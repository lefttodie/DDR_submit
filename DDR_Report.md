### 3. Probable Root Cause

The dampness observed at skirting levels across multiple rooms suggests a potential issue with rising damp or inadequate waterproofing. The tile hollowness in bathrooms may indicate poor adhesive application or water infiltration beneath the tiles. The structural crack on the external wall could be due to foundation movement or thermal expansion. Seepage in the parking area might result from poor drainage or cracks in the foundation. The plumbing issue in the common bathroom could be due to leaks or improper installation.

---

### 4. Severity Assessment

- **Dampness**: Moderate to High Severity - Widespread across living areas, potentially leading to mold growth and structural damage.
- **Tile Hollowness**: Moderate Severity - Affects bathroom safety and aesthetics.
- **Structural Crack**: High Severity - May compromise the building's integrity.
- **Seepage**: Moderate Severity - Could lead to water damage and foundation issues.
- **Plumbing Issue**: Moderate Severity - May cause water damage and affect bathroom functionality.

---

### 5. Recommended Actions

1. **Dampness**: Conduct a thorough inspection to identify the source of dampness. Apply waterproofing treatments and improve ventilation in affected areas.
2. **Tile Hollowness**: Replace hollow tiles and ensure proper adhesive application. Check for water leaks beneath tiles.
3. **Structural Crack**: Engage a structural engineer to assess the crack's severity and recommend appropriate repairs.
4. **Seepage**: Improve drainage systems and repair any cracks in the foundation or external walls.
5. **Plumbing Issue**: Inspect plumbing for leaks and repair or replace faulty components.

---

### 6. Additional Notes

- Regular maintenance and inspections are recommended to prevent further deterioration.
- Consider consulting with specialists for dampness and structural issues.
- Ensure proper ventilation and moisture control in living areas to mitigate dampness.
